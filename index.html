🛠️ Renovation Coordination Watchlist

⚡ Electrical & Neutral Wiring
  • Pull neutral wire to all wall boxes: Critical for Aqara H2 reliability and long-term smart switches.
  • Power to windows: Pull power for Living Room & Master Bed motorized curtains before ceiling plastering.
  • 4-wire AC power to Balcony: Required for motorized zip-track mounting and smart relay.
  • Power beside toilets: Required for Panasonic E-Bidet seats in both bathrooms.
  • Power points: Living sofa, robot dock base, water dispenser, study desk, bedside tables.
💧 Plumbing & Appliance Points
  • Aircon Piping: SWG 22 copper + 1/2" Armaflex insulation to prevent condensation.
  • Kitchen Washer/Dryer: Verify 600mm width, 850mm height, inlet, drain and door swing clearance.
  • Water Dispenser & Sink: Confirm counter cutout and under-sink water filter / leak sensor space.
  • Robot Vacuum Dock: Reserve power and water drain/inlet if opting for auto-refill dock.
📐 Carpentry & Acoustics
  • Floor soundproofing underlay: Must be installed before laying final flooring.
  • TV Console depth: Ensure enough clearance for Samsung HW-Q990F soundbar and cable conduits.
  • Climbing Gear storage: Custom ventilated metal cabinetry with internal airflow fans.
  • Curtain ceiling pelmets: Ensure double-track recess depth for motorized day/night curtains.
