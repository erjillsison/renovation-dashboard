🛠️ Renovation Coordination Watchlist

⚡ Electrical & Neutral Wiring
  • Pull neutral wire to all wall boxes: Critical for Aqara H2 reliability and long-term smart switches.
  • Power to windows: Pull power for Living Room & Master Bed motorized curtains before ceiling plastering.
  • 4-wire AC power to Balcony: Required for motorized zip-track mounting and smart relay.
  • Power beside toilets: Required for Panasonic E-Bidet seats in both bathrooms.
  • Power points: Living sofa, robot dock base, water dispenser, study desk, bedside tables.
💧 Plumbing & Appliance Points
  • Aircon Piping: SWG 22 copper + 1/2" Armaflex insulation to prevent condensation.
  • Kitchen Washer/Dryer: Verify 600mm width, 850mm height, inlet, drain and door swing clearance.
  • Water Dispenser & Sink: Confirm counter cutout and under-sink water filter / leak sensor space.
  • Robot Vacuum Dock: Reserve power and water drain/inlet if opting for auto-refill dock.
📐 Carpentry & Acoustics
  • Floor soundproofing underlay: Must be installed before laying final flooring.
  • TV Console depth: Ensure enough clearance for Samsung HW-Q990F soundbar and cable conduits.
  • Climbing Gear storage: Custom ventilated metal cabinetry with internal airflow fans.
  • Curtain ceiling pelmets: Ensure double-track recess depth for motorized day/night curtains.
🛋️ 1 mmWave Only (Living Room)
mmWave presence is strictly reserved for the sofa/TV lounge where people sit motionless for hours. Avoids $600+ cost, ghost wall penetration, and messy USB cords in other rooms.
🚿 "Wasp in a Box" (Bathrooms)
Uses battery Door Contact + PIR Motion. When motion is detected and the door closes, occupancy locks ON indefinitely. Lights NEVER shut off while showering or on the toilet!
⚡ Aqara H2 Decoupled Mode
Neutral-wire 230V mains is permanently supplied to smart CCT bulbs & fans. Physical switch presses trigger Zigbee automations without cutting power to downstream radios.
☀️ Adaptive Circadian Rhythm
Home Assistant shifts color temperatures seamlessly throughout the day (2200K amber candlelight → 5000K crisp daylight → 2700K warm relax) to protect sleep cycles.

# Home Assistant Template Binary Sensor: Bathroom Occupancy (Wasp in a Box)
# Combines Door Contact Sensor + PIR Motion Sensor
template:
  - binary_sensor:
      - name: "Master Bath Occupied"
        device_class: occupancy
        state: >
          {{ is_state('binary_sensor.master_bath_door', 'off') or is_state('binary_sensor.master_bath_motion', 'on') }}
        auto_off:
          seconds: 90

# Automation: Turn ON lights with Time-Conditioned Nightlight
automation:
  - alias: "Master Bath: Light Occupancy Trigger"
    trigger:
      - platform: state
        entity_id: binary_sensor.master_bath_motion
        to: "on"
    action:
      - choose:
          # Late-Night Piddle Mode (11PM - 6:30AM) -> Dim mirror light only
          - conditions:
              - condition: time
                after: "23:00:00"
                before: "06:30:00"
            sequence:
              - service: light.turn_on
                target:
                  entity_id: light.master_bath_mirror
                data:
                  brightness_pct: 15
                  color_temp_kelvin: 2200
        # Daytime / Normal -> Full Downlights
        default:
          - service: light.turn_on
            target:
              entity_id: light.master_bath_downlights
            data:
              brightness_pct: 100
              color_temp_kelvin: 4000
