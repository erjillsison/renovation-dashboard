# House Renovation 2026 — Procurement & Budget Visualizer

Interactive budget and procurement visualizer for Singapore condominium renovation (2026).

🌐 **Live Dashboard:** [https://erjillsison.github.io/renovation-dashboard/](https://erjillsison.github.io/renovation-dashboard/)

## Features
- **Itemized Procurement:** Selections across Whole House, Living Room, Kitchen, Master Bedroom, Bathrooms, Balcony, and Bedroom 2.
- **Interactive Budget Tracking:** Live toggle for seller contingency deductions, priority breakdown (P0/P1/P2), and estimated TBD ranges.
- **Smart Home & Lighting Schedule:** Room-by-room breakdown of smart wall switches (Aqara H2), smart CCT tunable bulbs, high-CRI downlights, and accent LED strips.
